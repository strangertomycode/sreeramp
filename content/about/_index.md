Hi, I'm Sreeram, a backend developer from Palakkad, Kerala, who recently completed an M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (AI & Software Engineering) at Cochin University of Science and Technology.

If you want the resume version of me, that's the home page. This page is the rest of it.

## Why This Site Exists

I built this site to actually be that: a personal corner of the internet, not just a portfolio with a bio bolted on.

The home page is where the professional stuff lives, the projects, the stack, the things I've built and can defend in an interview. But I wanted somewhere that wasn't only that.

## What You'll Find on the Blog

Some of it will be technical, notes on things I'm learning, building, or breaking.

Some of it won't be about code at all.

Not everything I write needs a target audience or a reason. Some of it can just be mine, published anyway.

## How It's Built

Everything here, including this page, is written and maintained from my own Obsidian vault.

Partly because it's the easiest way for me to actually keep this updated instead of letting it go stale, and partly because I thought it was cool that content in my vault will be directly published to my website.

## Where to Start

Poke around.

Read the blog if you want the less curated version of me, or stick to the home page if you just want to know whether I can build things that work.
