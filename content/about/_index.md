## **About**

I'm Sreeram P, and this is **words no one asked for**.

This is a personal space for the things I want to write down — thoughts, observations, stories, ideas, things I've learned, and things I'm still trying to figure out.

There isn't really a particular subject here. It's just a place to put words that would otherwise stay in my head.

If something here makes you think, laugh, disagree, or wonder why you read it in the first place, then I suppose the blog is doing its job.

## **This Blog**

I wanted a small, simple corner of the internet that feels like mine.

No promise that everything here will make sense.

Just words, whenever I have something worth saying.
