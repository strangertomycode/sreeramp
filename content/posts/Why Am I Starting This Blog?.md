Starting something doesn't mean I have it all figured out. 

I am creating this blog to figure out what I want. I hope writing about my thoughts will help me understand myself a little better and, eventually, figure out what my goals are.

The boredom one feel when they have nothing to do, hopelessness and the voices in your head that just never stay silent even for a second is what lead me to create this blog. 

It's like screaming at a cliff to vent your frustration, except I am screaming into the abyss of the internet.

I don't know what I want to do with this blog. I don't know what it will become. I wish to figure that out, and also figure my life out as I pour my mind in to this little corner of the internet.

If you are reading this, I welcome you to my own quiet spot on the internet. A place where I will be imperfect, where my thoughts might not always make sense, and where you might even question why you came here in the first place.

Thank you for reading 😄.
